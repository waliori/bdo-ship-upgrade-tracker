Every build in this app draws on one inventory: the one you actually have.
You start by queueing something you want to build.
Some ships can be reached more than one way, so it asks which.
It shows what each route costs. Neither of them is the right answer.
Queue a second thing, and the order matters. When stock is short, the build at the top gets it.
The Plan is everything the queue needs, gathered into one list.
Red is missing. Blue is still to make. Green is already covered.
As you gather, you type in what you have.
And every build re-plans around it, at once.
A whole trip can go in as one change, rather than a box at a time.
Anything you have the materials for can be made in the Workshop.
Crafting moves real stock. Ingredients out, the product in.
Slipped? Every change here steps back, and forward again.
Enhancing is kept separate, because attempts fail.
So it shows the real odds at your stack, and the most the part can ever cost you.
The Inventory is one tile per thing you own, or still need.
Open any of them and every way of getting it is priced:
what the shop wants, against what making one costs once its own ingredients are priced too, all the way down.
A part you already levelled in the game is recorded, not re-enhanced.
You tell it where the part got to, and it spends nothing to agree with you.
The Tree is there for the other question: why a build needs what it needs.
And To Get is the shopping list, grouped by where you would go for it.
That is the yard. What you want, what it takes, and what you still have to find.
