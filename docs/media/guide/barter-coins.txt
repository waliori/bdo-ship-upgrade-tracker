Bartering, part six. A day for Crow Coins.
This sailor has 2,000 Crow Coins, and their builds want nearly twenty thousand from the Crow Coin Shop. The storage at Iliya holds a few Level 3 and Level 4 goods.
Click "Crow Coins".
Every board has islands that pay in Crow Coins, and they take a Level 4 and nothing else. So a coin day is a climb to Level 4, cashed in.
The ladder shows it: Level 4 is cashed in, and the Crow Coins node beside it is the goal. On a silver day, that same node switches the day to coins.
A keep number at Level 4 is held back from the coin islands too. A Level 4 you keep is one you do not cash.
Climb to Level 4, cash it in, and how many chains on today’s board end at a coin island.
The chains.
The runs worth sailing are counted in coins now, and as a range: an island states a range, and the game pays one figure out of it. The most coins, the most an hour, the most for each unit of Parley.
The first chains start from what is held: one Level 4 from the Iliya storage, straight to a coin island. One trade each, since a coin island deals once a refresh.
Start from shows the other ways in: from the shore, or from the Level 3s in storage.
Take the most coins.
The run: the coins, as the islands state them, and how many islands pay.
The trades, and the coins each unit of Parley brings.
And the line under it, against your builds: what this run pays, how many coins are still short of what they want, and how many more runs like this one that is.
Load, sail and record it like any other run; the coins go into your purse with the trip. Next, a material day.
