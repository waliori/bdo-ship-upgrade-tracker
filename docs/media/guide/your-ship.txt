This is the Ship tab. Everything the app knows about how fast you sail comes from this one screen.
Start with the hull. Pick the ship you actually sail.
A ship has four part slots: figurehead, plating, cannon and sail.
Each one fills itself with the best part you own.
Click Change to pick a different one.
It lists every part that fits the slot, with what each does, and marks the ones you actually hold.
The fifth slot is your sea crystal.
Pick the one you have socketed, and its bonus goes into the numbers.
Below that is the appearance set.
Skins are cosmetic in most games. Here they are not: a full set adds real durability and rations, so tick the pieces you are wearing.
And this panel at the top is the ship itself: its speed, its hold limit, and how many of the five slots are filled.
The small print under each figure says where it came from — hull, parts, crystal, crew and mastery, added up.
Your Sailing Mastery goes in here, and those figures move with it.
Now the crew. This roster starts empty.
Typing eighteen sailors in, four growths each, is an evening you will not enjoy. So it reads them off your screenshots instead.
Screenshot the Manage Sailors window in game, one sailor a shot, and drop them in. It reads them in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
Check the table against the game, then take the ones that are right.
And there they are, hired, with their real levels and growths.
Sort the list by whatever you are fitting for. Best all round, best for speed, and so on.
To seat one by hand, click the sailor, then click the seat you want them in.
Where they sit matters. A sail seat doubles their speed growth, and the wheel doubles turn and brake.
Or let it arrange the whole crew for you. Click Auto assign.
It asks what the boat is for, and shows what each answer would actually give you.
Pick one, and it seats everybody.
And the row under the ship fills in. That is what the crew is adding, on top of the hull.
Two hot presets, for swapping crews quickly. Save the current one to a slot.
And click it to put that crew straight back on.
A setup is bigger: the whole fit-out, hull, parts, crystal and seating, under a name.
The fleet lists every setup you have kept, and every hull you own without one.
The one you are sailing is what the Map times its routes at.
And you can copy the whole thing as a link, if someone asks what you sail.
That is your ship. Get this screen right, and every distance and every minute in the app is yours.
