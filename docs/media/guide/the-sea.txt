The Yard says what you need. The Sea is where you go and get it.
Quests are what the sea gives you for free each day.
The ones paying something on your list are marked, so you can see which are worth the detour.
Tick a few off, then click Finish.
The rewards go into your stock as one change, which Undo can take back.
The ticks clear themselves at the daily reset.
The Map is that shopping list, drawn on the sea.
Every pin is a barterer holding something you are short of.
Switch to Route, and plot the loop.
It draws a route around the land, with the distance and the minutes on every leg —
worked out at the speed your ship actually sails.
This strip sets what the chart draws:
barterers, habitats, wharves, island names, and anything you drew yourself.
The Draw tab is for routes a shopping list cannot describe.
Click the sea to drop a numbered stop, in the order you will sail it.
Take the pen and drag to draw a line.
Or type a word straight onto the water.
It all sits on the chart, so it pans and zooms with everything else.
Save it under a name, share it as a link, or send it to the game map as bookmarks.
That is the Sea. What is out there, how far, and how long it takes.
