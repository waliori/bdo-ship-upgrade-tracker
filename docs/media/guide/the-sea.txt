The yard says what you need. The sea is where you go and get it.
Quests is what the sea hands you for free, every day.
The ones paying in something on your list are marked, so you can tell at a glance which are worth the detour.
Tick a few off, and Finish records them together.
The rewards land in your stock as one change, which undo can take back.
The ticks wear off at the daily reset by themselves.
The Map is that same shopping list, drawn on the sea.
Every pin is a barterer holding something you are short of.
Plot the loop, and it draws you one, bent round the land rather than through it.
Every leg carries its distance and its minutes,
worked out at the speed the ship you are actually sailing makes.
One strip along the top says what the chart draws:
barterers, habitats, wharves, island names, and anything you have drawn yourself.
Because some routes a shopping list cannot express.
Click the water for a numbered stop, in the order you mean to sail it.
Drag to draw a line, freehand.
Or type a word straight onto the sea.
It all lives on the chart, so it pans and zooms with everything else.
Keep it under a name, share it as a link, or send it to the game's own map as bookmarks.
That is the sea: what is out there, how far, and how long it takes to get to.
