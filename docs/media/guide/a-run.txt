Bartering is how most of these materials actually arrive. The Barter tab plans a day of it.
Every refresh, the whole sea shows one of forty layouts.
So you look at one island in the game, and tap what it is showing.
And the whole board follows: every chain the day allows, and what each of them pays.
The runs worth sailing are worked out for you. Tick the chains, and they are one run.
Which opens as a sheet: every stop, what to buy before you cast off, and the quests you pass on the way.
Sail it, and it is on the chart as a checklist, stop by stop.
Record the trip at the end, and the whole of it lands back in the Inventory,
as one change, against the builds that were waiting on it.
That is a run. The board answers, the route follows, and the yard gets what it asked for.
