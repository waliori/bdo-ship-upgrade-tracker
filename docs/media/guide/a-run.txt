Bartering is how most of what the yard wants actually arrives. This tab plans a whole day of it.
A run is for one of two things, and it asks which before anything else.
A material: the ladder up to one thing you are short of, counted against what is already aboard.
Or silver: every chain today's board allows, and a run along the ones you tick. That is the one to follow here.
Tell it your barter level, because that is what every Parley figure after this is priced at.
Now the board. Every refresh, the whole sea shows one of forty layouts.
It cannot know which. So it asks about a single island — the one whose offer tells the forty apart best.
The answer is on the game's own barter list, set from Normal to Level 1: find that island's row, and read across.
Tap the same thing here.
One look usually names the layout. Where two of the forty still fit, it says so, and asks for one more.
And the whole board follows from that one answer: every chain the day allows, and what each of them pays.
Next, what you want out of the day.

Watch the row along the bottom as it does: that is what the preset keeps back at each level — never sold, never spent below it, so tomorrow's board has something to start from.

The way round: nearest islands first whatever chain they belong to, or each chain climbed to its top before the next.
