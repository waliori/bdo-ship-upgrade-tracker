The Map's Route and Draw tabs: a loop through what you are short of, timed at your own ship, and routes of your own, drawn straight onto the sea.
Route turns the list of islands into a sailing route.
Plot the loop, and it joins every island you are short of, bent round the land rather than through it.
The tiles total it up: the stops, the distance and the time at your ship's speed, the hold, the rations it eats, and the Parley it costs.
Parley is counted at one trade a stop, or at every attempt the offer allows.
Every stop is a row: what that island has for you, and how the hold stands after it.
Say where you sail from, and whether you come back, and the times follow.
Click a stop, and the chart flies to it.
The cross takes a stop off, and a pin's card puts one back on.
The route is timed at the ship you sail. Switch setup times it at another one you have kept.
Reverse sails it the other way round.
Save keeps it by name. Eight are kept, and none is dropped without asking.
Copy link puts the route in an address. Signed in, it is a short one.
Export and Import carry it as a small file instead.
And Put it on the game's map writes the stops into Black Desert's own world map.
As favourites, named and numbered, or as one of the map's three navigation loops.
Copy the block, or download it, and paste it into gameVariable.xml at the character screen. Or let the app write it into the game's folder itself, keeping a copy of the old file.
Draw is for routes the list cannot describe.
Four tools: add stops, draw, write, and shade an area.
Add stops drops a numbered stop wherever you click, in the order you mean to sail. Legs bend round the land.
Pick another ink, and the next stops start a second line, numbered on its own.
Draw is a freehand line.
Write puts a note straight onto the water.
And shade an area marks a stretch of sea: click its corners, then the first one again.
Undo takes back the last mark, whatever kind it was.
Give it a name and a note.
Keep puts it on the shelf below.
Copy link carries the whole drawing, stops, lines, words and all, to anyone, with nothing of theirs touched.
To the game writes its lines into the game's map too, a loop each.
The shelf keeps the newest; the eye lays one over the chart beside what you are drawing.
And Browse opens the whole library, with a search and every drawing as a card.
That is routing and drawing. A loop timed at your own ship, and a way to show anyone else the sea you mean.
