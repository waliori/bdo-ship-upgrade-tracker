Everything so far runs in your browser alone. This last part is the one that needs sign-in.
One note first: the sailors on these boards are invented. A machine recording this has no server with real players on it. The tab, the digest and the ranking are the app's own.
Sixteen boards, built from the players who chose to join them.
They come in five sections: the sea, the runs, quests and hunts, the yard, and charts and hold.
The question mark on a board tells you exactly how that one is counted.
Your own places sit at the top, so you can see where you stand without hunting for yourself.
And there is a search, if you are after one sailor by name.
Click any place on a board to open that player's card.
Their fleet, their crew, their places, and what they have done at sea.
Click Look, and their ship loads onto your own Ship tab, fully fitted.
To look at, not to keep. One click puts yours back.
The other half of the tab adds the whole fleet up.
Which hulls people sail, which parts they fit, which crystals, which quests they run.
Search it for anything, and sort by most-first or A to Z.
It is the closest thing to an answer when you are wondering what everyone else fits.
Nothing goes onto a board that you did not offer.
You see the exact digest before you agree to it — the numbers, and nothing else.
And leaving takes it back off again.
Your data stays in your browser. Sign in to sync it between machines, or to join in. Neither is required for anything else in the app.
