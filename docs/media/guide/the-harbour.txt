Everything so far runs in your browser alone. This last part needs sign-in.
One note first: these sailors are made up. A machine recording this has no server with real players on it. The tab, the digest and the ranking are the app's own.
Sixteen boards, built from the players who chose to stand on them.
Click a place on a board to open that player's card.
Their fleet, their crew, and what they have done at sea.
Click Look, and their ship loads onto your own Ship tab, fully fitted.
To look at, not to keep. One click puts yours back.
The other half of the tab adds the whole fleet up.
Nothing goes onto a board that you did not offer.
You see the exact digest before you agree to it, and leaving deletes it again.
Your data stays in your browser. Sign in to sync it between machines, or to join in. Neither is required for anything else.
