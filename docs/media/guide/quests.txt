This is the Quests tab. It tracks the quests that pay in things you are already short of.
These are the sailing dailies and weeklies, all in one list.
The ones paying something on your build list are marked, so you can see which are worth the detour.
Filter them by what they pay, or by who gives them.
Each row shows what it pays, and how much of it you still need.
Some quests let you pick your reward. Those ask you which when you finish.
Tick off the ones you did in game.
Then click Finish.
The rewards go straight into your stock, as one change.
Got one wrong? Undo puts the whole batch back.
The ticks clear themselves at the daily reset, so you start each day fresh.
And these clocks tell you how long you have: dailies, weeklies, and the barter refresh.
Star the ones you always do, and they sort to the top.
Or save a set you run together, and tick the whole group at once.
Every quest knows where its giver stands, so a run can hand them in as it passes.
That is Quests. Free materials, tracked against what you are building.
