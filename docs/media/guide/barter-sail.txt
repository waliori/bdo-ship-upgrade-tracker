Bartering, part four. Under sail: one stop at a time, and the trip recorded at the end.
Light and fast from Iliya, on a bar just refilled, with everything the run needs ticked aboard.
Before you cast off, the clock’s chimes. A ship’s bell by default — click it to hear the next sound: three beeps, or none.
"Notify me too" asks the browser for a notification as well, so a chime lands while the game has the screen. Signed in, a chip beside it sends the chimes to every device on your account.
Click "Cast off".
The ship sets sail. The clock starts at this run’s own estimate, and the tab moves to the third step: one stop at a time.
This bar is the clock: the time to the next stop, and which stop it is.
It chimes at each stop — a pair of bells as each comes up, and eight bells at the end — or only once, for the whole run.
"Again" starts it over from now; "stop" puts it away.
The stop, in letters you can read across the room: the island, what you hand over, what you get back, and how many times.
Under it: the hold before and after, the Parley, and the rations.
If the Parley in the game differs, type what the game shows here, and the rest of the run is counted from it.
Trade in the game, then press Traded. The stop is written into the hold, and the next one comes up.
If you traded a different number of times, say so under it.
The hold, as it stands now and after this stop, with its slots.
"Time my legs" adds an Arrived button: press it as the ship reaches each island. After five legs, the clock uses your ship’s own speed.
Arrived.
The stop opens in glance mode, large. "Full view" brings the rest of the cockpit round it; "Glance mode" goes back.
"On the chart" opens the run on the Map, with the same checklist beside it.
Every stop is a numbered pin with the route drawn between them. The side panel carries the same stop and clock, and the strip at the foot steps through the legs.
Back to the Barter tab.
Finished sailing and did not tick as you went? Click "All done".
Tick off every stop and every quest handed in, at once.
Stops that pay a range are left for you, because only you saw what they paid.
Tap what each one paid, one after another. Each answer lays the rest of the run again, so the results add up to what you really got.
Paid more than the least, the run carries more goods, so it can grow by a few stops further on. Those are ticked the same way.
The results: stops, the trades made and your new Total Barters, the silver, the Parley spent and the time under way.
The exchange, as recording will write it: what you handed over, and what you received.
Click "Record the trip".
The Parley, Total Barters, the quests and the log move together, as one change.
So one Undo takes the whole trip back…
…and Redo puts it back again.
Below, today’s boards: every run since the refill, what it loaded and what it came back with, and the day’s totals.
What to load for the next board is not guessed: a refresh deals a different board, so it waits until you have read it.
The week, run by run, and every past run with all its stops.
That is a whole run. The next parts sail the same board for other things: a stock, Crow Coins, a material.
